## Introduction

Does an AI have a soul? Though this question may seem new, it has been a hot topic of debate between researchers, scientists and philosophers alike, but the question has never had a conclusive answer. Historically, though, the voice of reason had always leaned to the answer being in the negative. It was always assumed that humans have a unique element that made them inherently different from something artificial. In this project we discuss the historical development, business and societal perspective in regards to AI assistants. We will also discuss the ethics and the incremental improvement that led to something so close to the human experience that we might very well have an answer to the question. We chose this topic after extensive debate whether this was truly the topic we would genuinely have interest, though it was not the easiest decision considering there were much more appealing topics that could have resulted in 2 or 3 members of the group having a better time writing, but we ended up choosing topic such as this one because it hit closer to home in terms of our daily life and was more or less favored by all of our team members. It was also quite interesting since we were explicitly prohibited from using AI tools in the project, so this would be a purely human endeavor. Our goal for this project paper is to provide the reader with adequate knowledge about a technology that people use more and more of but know frighteningly little about, so they can examine for themselves the origins of AI to its current state.
