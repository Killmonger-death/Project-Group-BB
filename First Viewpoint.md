The McKinsey company states that work in the future will be a collaboration between people, agents and robots which will be powered by artificial intelligence. AI will transform entire workflows and what this means for skills however, AI will not make human skills useless but rather it will change how it is used. (Global Institute, 2025)
AI will start handling most common tasks in the workplace, which will make employees able to spend less time preparing documents and basic tedious research and more time on meaningful time framing questions and interpreting results. It is said the workforce of the future will be a collaboration of people, agents and robots. AI-powered automation will alter work, but people will remain indispensable. MIT’s state of AI in business 2025 report concludes that 95% of AI pilots are unsuccessful in delivering measurable business outcomes but this isn’t because of the AI’s inability to deliver results, at present this is due to the poor integration into current work systems.
ADVANTAGES OF AI INTERGRATION IN THE WORKPLACE
-	Improved decision making – AI enhances making decisions by quickly processing large volumes of structured and unstructured information, identifying models and distribution data and driven insights.
Examples of this would be, automating reports, scenario modeling, autonomous market analysts and dynamic pricing agents.
-	Increased efficiency – AI automates tedious time-consuming tasks often prone to human error, which will enable employees to focus on higher priority strategic projects.
Examples of this would be, email automation, content creation such as generating marketing material and document summarization.
-	Risk Management – AI enhances risk management by detection of patterns humans may miss; it also monitors compliance and detects fraud.
Examples of this would be, compliance monitoring, risk scenario generation and policy analysis.

AI assistants play constructive roles in government, business and consumer markets.
Introducing Artificial Intelligence Technologies into administration and operative processes can substantially reduce costs, increase efficiency, and automate routine tasks.
DISADVANTAGES OF AI INTERGRATION IN THE WORKPLACE
While AI can greatly benefit in the workplace, business owners still cannot neglect the risks of heavily relying on it. Extreme and unconstrained usage of Ai in the workplace poses the risk of negatively impacting cognitive skills, human connection which could leas to reduced employee well-being. 
Dr. Walther (2026) introduces the idea of not completely rejecting AI because of these potential risks but rather companies should take up a more hybrid intelligence, this is the combination of human literacy and algorithmic literacy.

They are automated software that makes decisions and executes operations based on predetermined criteria. They use artificial intelligence to tailor technology to personal needs. Companies utilise a wide range of technologies such as natural language processing that can imitate a person’s intelligence to resolve issues, and also machine learning. AI can then examine information, recognize patterns and learn from the data and adapt over time. 

AI in business holds the potential to enhance a wide range of business processes and domains, Over the next five years, business will speed up AI adoption by centering on areas where the technology is rapidly advancing.
For businesses, AI agents unlock significant advantages. They can manage end to end processes, operate around the clock and execute decisions at a speed and scale for beyond human capacity. AI has the potential to increase the consumer experience by offering personalised interactions, precise answers and a quicker service, but companies must learn to not neglect the foundations of the workplace, their employees, if we adopt a hybrid mindset and see the use of AI assistants as an extra tool and not a replacement for humans at work.
